ABOUT OUR COMPANY

Air9 Systems LTD is a registered Nigerian defence and security technology company incorporated on 18th December 2017.
We specialise in the procurement, integration, and delivery of critical military equipment, surveillance technologies, and tactical
platforms. Air9 Systems LTD bridges the gap between national defence needs and advanced military-grade solutions through strategic global partnerships and proven project execution.
